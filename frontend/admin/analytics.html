Utilization is an estimate based on recorded session start and end times and the current installed charger count. Chargers added or removed during the selected period may affect the estimate. Waiting time uses completed allocations with a recorded request arrival time; energy totals include sessions where energy was recorded. Revenue sums successful advance and legacy session payments by station; failed attempts are excluded.
